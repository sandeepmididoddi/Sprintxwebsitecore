Our Accelerator Programs

Early Stage

For startups with an MVP looking to validate their product-market fit

  • 10-week program
  • 10L seed funding
  • Mentorship from industry experts

Our Early Stage program is designed to help you refine your MVP, validate your market, and prepare for rapid growth. You'll work closely with experienced mentors and receive the initial funding needed to take your startup to the next level.

Growth Stage

For startups with traction looking to scale their operations

  • 12-week program
  • Up to 1M$ funding
  • Access to global markets

The Growth Stage program is tailored for startups ready to scale. We provide substantial funding, connections to global markets, and strategic guidance to help you achieve exponential growth and become a market leader.

AI & Blockchain

Specialized program for AI and blockchain startups

  • 20-week program
  • Up to $500k funding
  • Cutting-edge resources and partnerships

Our AI & Blockchain program offers specialized support for startups in these cutting-edge fields. Gain access to advanced resources, industry-specific mentors, and partnerships with leading tech companies to accelerate your innovative solutions.
